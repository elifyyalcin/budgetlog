Privacy Policy

App: BudgetLog

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BudgetLog handles information when you use the application.

1. Information You Enter

The app may allow you to enter income, expenses, transaction amounts, descriptions, categories, budgets, dates, and app preferences. This information is used only to provide the app's personal finance and budgeting features.

2. Accounts

BudgetLog does not require you to create an account or sign in.

3. Data Storage

Financial and budgeting information entered into the app is stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated in the app. We do not have access to locally stored transaction, income, expense, category, or budget data.

4. Sensitive Financial Information

You should not enter credit card numbers, bank account passwords, online banking credentials, PINs, or authentication codes into the app. The app does not require this information.

5. Analytics and Tracking

BudgetLog does not use third-party analytics or advertising tracking technologies unless explicitly disclosed in this Privacy Policy. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Advertising

The current version of BudgetLog does not display third-party advertising.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may use Apple services necessary for normal iOS functionality, distribution, purchases, backups, or system services. If additional third-party services are added, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share financial information entered into the app with third parties unless it is necessary to provide a feature you explicitly request, required by law, or explicitly initiated by you.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it using the app's available deletion features or remove the app from your device. If synchronization or backup services are enabled, deletion behavior may differ.

10. Children's Privacy

BudgetLog is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Security

We take reasonable measures when designing the app to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage system or device can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
